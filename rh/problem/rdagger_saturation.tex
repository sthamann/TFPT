\title{\bfseries The saturated dual resolvent\\[2mm]
\large A specialist dossier on the critical bordered block
$\boldsymbol{R^{\dagger}}$}
\author{Stefan Hamann}
\date{August 29, 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}\noindent
On a family of explicit finite discrete measures, a particle--hole
duality identifies a two-measure subordination inequality with the
spectral condition $R\succ\half I$ on a dual hole kernel.  A rank-one
border of that kernel produces a single object $\Rd$ whose positivity
$\Rd\succ\half I$ is equivalent to the original inequality
\emph{and} a terminal budget condition, and whose critical
three-by-three Schur block carries both.  A constrained equilibrium
problem on the same dual weight places the binding pair of nodes
astride a saturated hard-edge block.  All identities below are finite
linear algebra; all numerical values are a frozen census on a finite
family.  No asymptotic theorem is proved.  After the two-rank inertia
cut and the matrix-Weyl sequence (rounds 367--373) the one open
question is the \emph{minimal inertia form}
Problem~\ref{prob:minimal}: an integer mode count and the sign of an
$O(1)$ $2\times2$ source, not a margin asymptotic.  The older
saturation/RHP question is retained as the stronger variant
Problem~\ref{prob:rhp}.  This document is a problem statement for
specialists, not a claim, and nothing in it is evidence for or
against the Riemann hypothesis.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup}\label{sec:setup}

Fix a prime power $z$ (the window anchor).  A triangular (``tent'')
test function of half-width $\Delta$, determined by $z$ and a fixed
oversampling parameter, samples the von Mangoldt comb and an
archimedean kernel, producing an even trigonometric polynomial
$f$ --- the symbol of a circulant.  Folding the symbol onto $[-1,1]$
by $x_{j}=\cos\theta_{j}$ yields a signed discrete measure on the
cosine grid, split by sign into two positive measures $\mu$ and
$\nu$ with disjoint supports in $[-1,1)$ and $S$ atoms in total
\cite[construction]{lstar-note}.  Write $N=\lceil S/2\rceil$ and
$Y=\operatorname{supp}\nu$.  Across the whole family the grid is
unculled, so $S=2N-1$ \gr{exact}: half-filling is Borodin's
complementary rank condition, not an extra hypothesis.

The running instance is $z=16$: $S=367$, $N=184$, $|Y|=104$.  The
binding pair of $\nu$-atoms sits at grid (fold) indices $(2,4)$,
the two rightmost odd nodes of the cosine lattice.  The inequality
under study, denoted $\Ls$, asks
\[
\int p^{2}\dd\nu \;<\; \int p^{2}\dd\mu
\qquad\text{for every real polynomial }p\neq 0\text{ with }
\deg p<N.
\]
Equivalently, the $\nu$-sampling matrix $\E_{N}$ in the
$\mu$-orthonormal frame satisfies $\lambda_{\max}(\E_{N})<1$.  An
augmented form $\Ld$ adjoins a rank-one border (a budget-normalized
smooth test functional) and asks $\lambda_{\max}(\E_{N}+bb^{T}/B)<1$.
Both statements are open for the unbounded family.  On every tested
window the inequalities hold, with spectral margins decaying as a
power of $N$; structurally destroyed comparison inputs fail already
at a small fraction of the critical degree \cite{lstar-note}.  No
statement here claims either inequality.

\section{Definition of \texorpdfstring{$\boldsymbol{R^{\dagger}}$}{R-dagger}}
\label{sec:def}

Lift $(\mu,\nu)$ to the positive measure $\eta=\mu+\nu$ with weights
$u_{j}=|\widetilde w_{j}|$ on the union $X=\operatorname{supp}\eta$.
Let $P_{X}$ be the monic node polynomial of $X$.  Borodin's reciprocal
weight \cite{Borodin2002} is
$u^{\vee}_{j}=1\bigl/(u_{j}\,P_{X}'(x_{j})^{2}\bigr)$.  On this
cosine grid the dual weight has the closed form
\begin{equation}\label{eq:uvee}
u^{\vee}_{j}\;\propto\;
\frac{c_{j}\,(1-x_{j})}{|f(\theta_{j})|}\,,
\end{equation}
where $c_{j}$ is the folding weight ($c_{j}=\tfrac12$ at the left
endpoint, else $1$): the original weight carries $+\log|f|$, the dual
weight $-\log|f|$ \gr{exact, three evaluation routes}.  Let
$\Pi_{N-1}^{u^{\vee}}$ be the rank-$(N-1)$ Christoffel--Darboux
projection kernel of $u^{\vee}$ on $X$, dressed by
$\sqrt{u^{\vee}}$, and let $R$ be its restriction to $Y$.

Let $b$ be the border functional in the $\mu$-orthonormal frame,
$B>0$ the budget, $\beta=b/\sqrt{B}$, and $v$ the node-side image of
the border under the $\mu$-Christoffel--Darboux kernel
($v_{j}=\sqrt{\nu_{j}/B}\int K_{\mu}(y_{j},t)\,\dd\sigma(t)$).  Write
$\gamma=\beta^{T}\beta$ and $\widetilde v=Dv$, with $D$ the diagonal
sign matrix of $P_{X}'$.

\begin{definition}[Bordered dual resolvent]\label{def:rdagger}
The \emph{augmented dual kernel} is the $(|Y|+1)\times(|Y|+1)$ matrix
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rdagger}
\Rd
\;=\;
\begin{pmatrix}
R^{-1} & \widetilde v \\
\widetilde v^{T} & 1+\gamma
\end{pmatrix}^{-1}.
\end{equation}
The extra coordinate is the border; the leading principal block is a
rank-one Sherman--Morrison deformation of $R$
(Proposition~\ref{prop:A}\,(A4) below).
\end{definition}

Route~(i) --- adjoining a virtual atom and taking the
$(S+1)$-point Borodin kernel --- is available on a single-atom toy
border (half-filling shifts $S=2N-1\mapsto S^{\dagger}=2N$, dual rank
$N-1\mapsto N$) and agrees with \eqref{eq:rdagger} up to sign
conjugation \gr{exact, rational arithmetic}.  On the real windows the
border is a signed smooth-comb measure with $S$ atoms, every one of
which collides with a grid node, and is not a point evaluation
(representability spread $56.8$ on the running instance).  The
bordered-resolvent form is therefore the general object.

\section{Exact duality formulae}\label{sec:duality}

Write $\operatorname{margin}=1-\lambda_{\max}(\E_{N})$ and
$\operatorname{margin}^{\dagger}=1-\lambda_{\max}(\E_{N}+bb^{T}/B)$.

\begin{prop}[Particle--hole dictionary \cite{Borodin2002}]\label{prop:borodin}
At half-filling $S=2N-1$,
\[
\Ls
\;\iff\;
\lambda_{\max}(\E_{N})<1
\;\iff\;
R\;\succ\;\half I\,,
\]
with the spectral map
$\operatorname{margin}=2-1/\lambda_{\min}(R)$
\gr{theorem-grade; absolute deviation $\le 9.9\cdot10^{-10}$ on
$85$ windows}.  The $L$-ensemble transformation
$Q=\E_{N}(I+\E_{N})^{-1}$ is exact
\gr{rational arithmetic on a $5$-atom model; double precision
$\le 7.6\cdot10^{-12}$ on $85$ windows}, and combined with Borodin's
complementation $\Pi_{N}^{u}=D\,(I-\Pi_{N-1}^{u^{\vee}})\,D$ yields
$\E_{N}=D(R^{-1}-I)D$ on $Y$.
\end{prop}

Live census: $\lambda_{\min}(R)-\half$ ranges from $+4.2\cdot10^{-5}$
(running instance: $\lambda_{\min}(R)=0.500041882$) down to
$+5.9\cdot10^{-11}$ on the deepest tranche --- all positive, no
counterexample.  Comparison inputs with destroyed source structure
violate $R\succ\half I$ structurally
($\lambda_{\min}(R)-\half\in[-0.500,-0.083]$).  Honest limit: at the
deepest instances the double-precision resolution of the dual route
($\sim5\cdot10^{-10}$) exceeds the margins, so the hole coordinate is
exact algebra, not a sharper certification instrument.

The pair identities $c=\varepsilon_{1}\varepsilon_{2}(R^{-1})_{12}$,
$p=2-(R^{-1})_{11}$, $q=2-(R^{-1})_{22}$ identify the two-point
Uvarov block of the companion note with the $(1,2)$ principal minor
of $2I-R^{-1}$ \gr{theorem-grade}.  The absolutely continuous
hard-edge class is excluded at census grade: the rescaled pair
positions $a_{j}=2N^{2}(1-y_{j})$ are family-constant against
$\pi^{2}j^{2}/4$ on all $85$ windows (maximum deviation
$0.33/N$), while the kernel correlation $\rho_{K}$ decays with
fitted exponent $1.4222$, contrary to Lubinsky's Bessel limit at
fixed microscopic arguments \cite{Lubinsky2008},
\cite[\S{}frozen-placement]{lstar-note}.

\begin{prop}[Augmented dictionary]\label{prop:A}
Let
$Z=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}R^{-1}&\widetilde v\\
\widetilde v^{T}&1+\gamma\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$,
so $\Rd=Z^{-1}$, and write $M^{\dagger}=\Rd-\half I$.  The following
are finite-matrix identities \gr{theorem-grade; rational arithmetic
on toys, graded double precision on $75$ windows of the prime-generated
family plus two character-twisted families of $42$ windows each}.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(A1)}]
The augmented node Gram $G^{\dagger}$ is the sign-conjugate of
$(\Rd)^{-1}-I$: the particle--hole complementation extends by one
row.
\item[\textup{(A2)}]
$\operatorname{margin}^{\dagger}=2-1/\lambda_{\min}(\Rd)$
(deviation $\le 1.1\cdot10^{-11}$ shallow,
$\le 5.5\cdot10^{-10}$ at the deepest computed row).
\item[\textup{(A3)}]
$\Ld\;\iff\;\Rd\succ\half I$, two-sided.  Both truth directions
occur on real windows: six character-twisted rungs with terminal
ratio $q_{N}>1$ have $\lambda_{\min}(\Rd)-\half<0$ (e.g.\ $-9.8\cdot
10^{-5}$), exactly as the identity demands.
\item[\textup{(A4)}]
Sherman--Morrison on the $Y$-block:
$\Rd_{YY}=R+(R\widetilde v)(R\widetilde v)^{T}/
(1+\gamma-\widetilde v^{T}R\widetilde v)$,
and $\Rd_{bb}=1/(1+\gamma-\widetilde v^{T}R\widetilde v)$
(deviation $\le 1.2\cdot10^{-14}$ on every computed row, including
the deepest).  The border is a rank-one insertion at dual-resolvent
level.
\item[\textup{(A5)}]
$\Rd\succ\half I$ if and only if $R\succ\half I$ and $q^{\dagger}<1$,
where $q^{\dagger}=\gamma+v^{T}(I-\E_{N})^{-1}v$ is frame-invariant
(equals the squared dual border norm $u^{T}H^{-1}u/B$) and coincides
with the standing terminal coordinate.  The dictionary
$1-q^{\dagger}=(5/7)(1-q_{N})/B$ holds to relative
$6.7\cdot10^{-6}$.
\item[\textup{(A6)}]
The Schur complement of $M^{\dagger}$ onto the border coordinate
equals $(1-q^{\dagger})/\bigl(2(1+q^{\dagger})\bigr)$ globally, and
equals the same value inside the local $3\times3$ block of
Proposition~\ref{prop:block} (quotient $\le 1.0\cdot10^{-11}$).
\item[\textup{(A7)}]
Cauchy interlacing:
$\lambda_{k}(\Rd)\le\lambda_{k}(R)\le\lambda_{k+1}(\Rd)$.
The border can only lower the bottom of the dual spectrum
($\operatorname{margin}^{\dagger}\le\operatorname{margin}$).
On the running instance,
$0.500041459\le 0.500041882$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{prop}

A formal reconstruction, independent of the dual coordinate, is
already machine-checked in Lean: $\Ld\iff\Ls\wedge\mathrm{Terminal}$
and $\Ls\wedge\mathrm{Terminal}\Rightarrow$ positivity of every
bordered window matrix through the cap
(\texttt{RH/Augmented.lean}, \texttt{RH/Closure.lean};
axiom audit in \texttt{RH/Audit.lean}, consuming only the two named
canonical holes).  The dual-resolvent dictionary itself is
sorry-free since round 373: $\Ld\iff\Rd\succ\half I$ under the
$\mu$-ONB whitening (\texttt{RH/DualResolvent.lean})
\gr{lean-verified}.  Haynsworth additivity on the two-rank and mixed
$J$-forms is likewise sorry-free
(\texttt{RH/Haynsworth.lean}; Section~\ref{sec:minimal}).
What is \emph{not} formalized, and not proved analytically, is the
pair of census premises (P1)--(P2) of Problem~\ref{prob:minimal} for
the unbounded family --- and, a fortiori, $\Rd\succ\half I$ itself.

\section{The critical three-by-three block}\label{sec:block}

Partition $Y=\{y_{1},y_{2}\}\cup C$, with $\{y_{1},y_{2}\}$ the
binding pair (folds $2,4$) and $C$ the rest of $Y$.  Write
$M=R-\half I$ and $S_{N}$ for the $2\times2$ Schur complement of $M$
onto the pair.  Then \gr{exact}
\[
M\succ0
\;\iff\;
M_{CC}\succ0
\;\text{ and }\;
S_{N}\succ0\,,
\]
and $S_{N}$ is a ratio of Sylvester-bordered minors of $\det M$
(the rest block sits inside the border; a pair-only truncation is
false by $4.07$ nats on the running instance and exactly on the
rational toy).  The off-diagonal of the dual resolvent
$A^{-1}=(I-2R)^{-1}$ is the Casoratian of the two consecutive dual
orthonormal polynomials, resolvent-dressed (discrete IIKS identity)
\gr{theorem-grade}.  The diagonal entries $(A^{-1})_{kk}$ are
\emph{not} Casoratian data: their cross share
$(A^{-1})_{12}^{2}/\bigl((A^{-1})_{11}(A^{-1})_{22}\bigr)$ has
median $0.702$ on $85$ windows (range $0.46$--$0.84$).  No standalone
explicit prefactor turns the Casoratian into $\det S_{N}$ at the
sealed bars.

\begin{prop}[Binding]\label{prop:block}
On the $74$ resolvable windows of the family
($\lambda_{\min}(R)-\half>10^{-9}$),
\[
\operatorname{bind}
\;=\;
\frac{\lambda_{\min}(S_{N})}{\lambda_{\min}(R)-\half}
\in[1.0003,\,1.0605]\,,
\quad\text{median }1.0058
\]
\gr{census}.  After the rank-one border, the $3\times3$ Schur block
$S^{\dagger}_{3}$ of $M^{\dagger}$ on (pair, border) satisfies
\[
\operatorname{bind}^{\dagger}
\;=\;
\frac{\lambda_{\min}(S^{\dagger}_{3})}{\lambda_{\min}(\Rd)-\half}
\in[1.0003,\,1.0608]\,,
\quad\text{median }1.0056
\]
\gr{census}.  The in-block border Schur equals the global border
Schur of \textup{(A6)}.  The rank-one deformation does not move the
pair structure: on the running instance the cross share of $\Rd$ is
$0.6990$ against $0.6973$ for $R$; ladder medians $0.708$ against
$0.702$.
\end{prop}

On the running instance the three eigenvalues of $S^{\dagger}_{3}$ are
$(4.16\cdot10^{-5},\,4.90\cdot10^{-4},\,1.76\cdot10^{-2})$: the bottom
mode is the $\Ls$ pair channel, the top mode is the terminal border
channel.  The critical eigenvector's border mass is
$2.5\cdot10^{-5}$ there, and $5.8\cdot10^{-4}$ at the
near-terminal window where
$\operatorname{margin}^{\dagger}/\operatorname{margin}=0.8716$.
The giant operator behaves, at the critical point, like this
$3\times3$ block.

\section{The pair straddles the saturation edge}\label{sec:edge}

Two independent finite objects describe the dual ensemble's occupation
of the cosine grid.

\paragraph{Exact occupation.}
Let $o_{\eta}(x_{j})$ (resp.\ $o_{\mathrm{dual}}(x_{j})$) be the
diagonal of the rank-$N$ $\eta$-projection (resp.\ the rank-$(N-1)$
dual-hole projection).  Borodin complementation on the diagonal gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:occ}
o_{\eta}(x_{j})+o_{\mathrm{dual}}(x_{j})\;=\;1
\end{equation}
at every union node, with trace ward
$\sum_{j}o_{\mathrm{dual}}(x_{j})=N-1$
\gr{exact; rational arithmetic on the toy, $\le 2.2\cdot10^{-11}$
live}.  This is the finite counterpart of an equilibrium density
versus its upper constraint, with no solver and no asymptotics.  On
the prime-generated family, fold~$1$ (the rightmost node) is the
unique occupation anomaly: $o_{\mathrm{dual}}(x_{1})$ lies
$0.25$--$0.33$ below the bulk median on all $85$ windows, while the
pair folds $2$ and $4$ are flat (deviation $\le 0.10$).

\paragraph{Obstacle problem.}
Minimize the discrete energy
\[
E(m)
=\sum_{j\neq k}m_{j}m_{k}\log\frac{1}{|x_{j}-x_{k}|}
+\sum_{j}m_{j}\bigl(-\log u^{\vee}_{j}\bigr)
\]
over $0\le m_{j}\le 1$, $\sum m_{j}=N-1$.  The box constraint
\emph{is} the Baik--Kriecherbauer--McLaughlin--Miller upper constraint
\cite{BKMM2007}; dropping it piles mass $\ge 92$ times the cap onto
single nodes.  A deterministic projected first-order solver
(FISTA plus exact box-simplex water-filling, sealed iteration
counts, KKT partition gates) produces, on four sealed instances
including the running instance, a dual-void block equal to folds
$\{1,2,3\}$ \gr{census}.  By \eqref{eq:occ} this is the primal
saturated block at the right hard edge.

\begin{prop}[Straddle, census]\label{prop:straddle}
The binding pair $(2,4)$ straddles the saturation-block edge $3\mid 4$:
fold $2$ lies inside the saturated zone, fold $4$ outside.
Auxiliary character-twisted families on the same grid have a
\emph{different} partition --- they occupy fold $1$, with no fold-$1$
occupation anomaly on $0/42+0/42$ windows --- and a sign-scrambled
comparison input is disordered at the edge while failing
$R_{CC}\succ\half I$ ($\lambda_{\min}(R_{CC})-\half=-0.4962$) with
the pair block itself still positive.  The identity chain is
world-blind algebra; the partition, and the positivity, are not.
\end{prop}

The asymptotic equilibrium-measure theorem (BKMM band and saturation
limit shape for the field $-\log u^{\vee}$) is not proved
\gr{hypothesis, open}.  The finite partition is a measurement about
this family.

\section{The observed exponent}\label{sec:alpha}

On the $57$ calibration windows a Theil--Sen fit of
$\log\lambda_{\min}(S_{N})$ has slope $-3.334$, hitting the standing
margin exponent $3.332$ at distance $0.002$ and rejecting an
alternative reading $1.65$ at distance $1.684$ \gr{census}.  The
diagonal dual-resolvent entries carry the same exponent
($\lvert(A^{-1})_{11}\rvert$ slope $+3.365$,
$\lvert(A^{-1})_{22}\rvert$ $+3.292$,
$\lvert(A^{-1})_{12}\rvert$ $+3.316$).  Weight-side Christoffel
exponents ($1.79$ and $0.38$ families) miss by a wide margin: they
do not carry the resolvent diagonals.

A dimensionless combination
$t_{\mathrm{geo}}
=(\lambda_{\min}(R)-\half)\,
\sqrt{(A^{-1})_{11}(A^{-1})_{22}}$
is margin-locked: slope $-0.009$ on the $57$, range
$[0.222,\,0.287]$ on all $74$ resolvable windows \gr{census}.  Exact
algebra identifies $t_{\mathrm{geo}}$ with a function of
$\det(S_{N}/\varepsilon)$ and the cross share; family-constancy is a
local-parametrix-class census, not a Gamma-function derivation.
The Gamma parametrix of \cite[Thm~2.7, Rem~2.8]{BKMM2007} adjacent
to a saturated band remains the natural analytic candidate
\gr{hypothesis, open}.

An adjugate identity
$\det S_{N}\cdot 4\bigl[(A^{-1})_{11}(A^{-1})_{22}-(A^{-1})_{12}^{2}\bigr]
=1$
\gr{exact} implies that the wander of $\log\det S_{N}$ \emph{is}
the wander of $-\log\bigl((A^{-1})_{11}(A^{-1})_{22}\bigr)$
(fine-structure correlation $+0.9835$), with the cross-share term
five times subleading.  The leading common wander lives in the
diagonal resolvent data and cancels algebraically in the minor.

\section{Relative error-operator form}\label{sec:error}

Two constant-matrix targets fail the census and force a relative
statement.

\paragraph{Rest block.}
Cauchy interlacing on the principal submatrix $R_{CC}$, and the
Schur-complement eigenvalue inequality, give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rest}
\lambda_{\min}(R_{CC})-\half
\;\ge\;
c\,\bigl(\lambda_{\min}(R)-\half\bigr)
\qquad\text{with }c=1
\end{equation}
as a finite theorem, gated in both directions on the rational toy
and on $74+84$ resolvable windows \gr{theorem-grade}.  On the
prime-generated family the measured ratio lies in $[8.8,\,54.2]$
(median $20.3$).  On the character-twisted families the ratio
reaches the interlacing line: minima $1.0$ on $11$ of $84$ windows.
Any analytic rest-block clause is a theorem only at $c=1$; a
comfortable factor $8.8$ does not transfer.

\paragraph{Leading coefficient.}
The schematic
$S_{N}=N^{-\alpha}(M_{0}+O(N^{-\varepsilon}))$
with $M_{0}$ a constant positive matrix is too rigid.  The condition
number $\kappa_{S}=\lambda_{\max}(S_{N})/\lambda_{\min}(S_{N})$
ranges in $[6.3,\,30.5]$ (median $13.9$), a range ratio $4.88>1.5$:
the margin direction collapses ($\operatorname{bind}$ median
$1.0058$) but the second eigenvalue wanders by an $O(1)$ factor
\gr{census}.  The honest form is projective: one controls
$S_{N}/\lambda_{\min}(S_{N})$ in a bounded set of positive-definite
matrices, or equivalently the error operator
$E=P_{\mathrm{true}}P_{\mathrm{model}}^{-1}-I$
of a local Riemann--Hilbert parametrix in a \emph{relative} operator
norm on the critical block, not as an additive constant remainder.

No bound on $E$ is proved \gr{open}.  Equation~\eqref{eq:rest} at
$c=1$ is the only theorem-grade error control presently available,
and it is finite interlacing, not an asymptotic remainder.  After
rounds 367--373 this relative form is the \emph{stronger} target:
the primary question no longer needs a remainder that tracks
$N^{-\alpha}$ (Section~\ref{sec:minimal}).

\section{The minimal inertia form (rounds 367--373)}
\label{sec:minimal}

The schematic $S_{N}=N^{-\alpha}(M_{0}+O(N^{-\varepsilon}))$ of
Section~\ref{sec:error} is a margin asymptotic.  The two-rank
inertia cut replaces it by two coarser statements on the rest block
$A_{0}=R_{N-3}-\half I$ and the $2\times2$ Schur matrix
$K_{2}=I+U^{T}A_{0}^{-1}U$, with $U$ the last two dual
Christoffel--Darboux columns.

\paragraph{Analytic discount.}
Write $\operatorname{ind}_{-}(A)$ for the number of strictly
negative eigenvalues of a real Hermitian matrix $A$.  It is enough
to prove, uniformly on the admissible family,
\begin{align}
\operatorname{ind}_{-}(A_{0})
&\;\le\;1
\tag{P1}\\
\det K_{2}
&\;\le\;-c_{0}<0
\quad\text{on the branch }\operatorname{ind}_{-}(A_{0})=1.
\tag{P2}
\end{align}
(P1) is an integer mode count.  (P2) is the sign of an $O(1)$
$2\times2$ source.  An asymptotic argument need only beat a
\emph{fixed} signature reserve, not the shrinking
$N^{-3.33}$ spectral margin of Section~\ref{sec:alpha}.

Census on the $74$ resolvable windows
($\lambda_{\min}(R)-\half>10^{-9}$) \gr{census, round 367}:
overload $\operatorname{ind}_{-}(A_{0})\ge2$ is $0/74$; the
dichotomy is $45$ exact-one (nneg-$1$) against $29$ already
positive-definite at rank $N-3$ (P1 vacuous).  On the nneg-$1$
branch, $-\det K_{2}\in[1.157,\,1126.389]$, census floor $0.50$
holds $45/45$, Theil--Sen slope of $\log(-\det K_{2})$ against
$\log N_{w}$ is $+0.543$ (flat against the margin rate $-3.332$),
and the restatement correlation against $\log\operatorname{margin}$
on the $57$ calibration windows is $-0.0064$.  The implication
$\mathrm{(P1)}\wedge\mathrm{(P2)}\Rightarrow R\succ\half I$ holds on
all $74$ resolvable rows; it is sufficient, not necessary (the
vacuous-$29$ already have $A_{0}\succ0$).

\begin{thm}[Two-rank Haynsworth]
\label{thm:haynsworth}
Let $A_{0}$ be real Hermitian and invertible with
$\operatorname{ind}_{-}(A_{0})=1$, and let
$K_{2}=I_{2}+U^{T}A_{0}^{-1}U$.  If $\det K_{2}<0$, then
$A_{0}+UU^{T}\succ0$
\gr{lean-verified, \texttt{RH/Haynsworth.lean}, sorry-free}.
\end{thm}

Finite real algebra only; the census premises (P1)--(P2) on windows
are \emph{not} asserted.  The Lean statement is

\section{Problem statement}\label{sec:problem}

\begin{problem}[Minimal inertia of the rest block]
\label{prob:minimal}
Let $(\mu,\nu)$ run over the admissible family of
\cite{lstar-note}, with dual kernel $R$ as in
Section~\ref{sec:def}, rest block $A_{0}=R_{N-3}-\half I$, and
$K_{2}=I+U^{T}A_{0}^{-1}U$ the two-rank Schur matrix of
Section~\ref{sec:minimal}.  Prove \textup{(P1)} and \textup{(P2)}
uniformly for the unbounded family: at most one negative inertia
direction in $A_{0}$, and $\det K_{2}\le -c_{0}<0$ on the
nneg-$1$ branch.  Which theorem in the Riemann--Hilbert analysis
of discrete orthogonal polynomials --- in particular, which
statement in the saturation regime of
Baik--Kriecherbauer--McLaughlin--Miller
\emph{\cite[Thm~2.7, Remark~2.8]{BKMM2007}} --- supplies these two
coarser facts?  The target is an integer mode count and the sign
of an $O(1)$ minor, not a rate.
\end{problem}

By Theorem~\ref{thm:haynsworth} a proof of
Problem~\ref{prob:minimal} gives $R\succ\half I$.  The R$^{\dagger}$
layer then lifts to $\Ld$ once the terminal Schur is in hand
(Proposition~\ref{prop:A}(A3),(A5), Lean-verified).  The mixed
identity \eqref{eq:mixed} is the bordered analogue: the same
Haynsworth additivity, with $J=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,1/\mathrm{den})$,
applies to $M^{\dagger}$.

\begin{problem}[Stronger saturation form]
\label{prob:rhp}
In the notation of Definition~\ref{def:rdagger}, which local
parametrix at a hard edge adjacent to a saturated band guarantees
\begin{equation}\label{eq:target}
\Rd\;\succ\;\half I
\end{equation}
uniformly, equivalently that the critical $3\times3$ Schur block
of $\Rd-\half I$ (binding pair plus border) stays
positive-definite?  This is the pre-367 target: a margin
asymptotic $S_{N}=N^{-\alpha}(M_{0}+\cdots)$ with relative error
control.  It implies Problem~\ref{prob:minimal} and is strictly
stronger.
\end{problem}

The finite input to either theorem is assembled above: reciprocal
weight \eqref{eq:uvee}; occupation duality \eqref{eq:occ}; a
census-grade saturated block $\{1,2,3\}$ with the binding pair
astride the edge $3\mid4$; the dichotomy
$\operatorname{ind}_{-}(A_{0})\le1$ on $74/74$ with (P2) on the
$45$-row nneg-$1$ branch; mixed form \eqref{eq:mixed}; observed
rate $N^{-3.332}$ (now optional); local-parametrix datum
$t_{\mathrm{geo}}$ margin-locked in $[0.222,0.287]$; error
control in relative/projective form, with rest-block theorem only
at $c=1$.  The AC/Bessel class is the wrong class
\cite{Lubinsky2008}.  A Gamma-class derivation at saturation
adjacency remains a candidate for the \emph{stronger} form and is
open.  The cheaper target is (P1)--(P2).

\section{The graph-resolvent form (rounds 407--412)}\label{sec:graph}

Rounds 407, 409 and 411 produced three equivalent finite-algebra
dictionaries for the rest-block count (P1).  Round 412 records them

\texttt{rh/lean/RH/GraphResolvent.lean}.  Nothing below asserts (P1)
or $\Rd\succ\half I$ on the family; the mincut remains the named

\paragraph{Dictionary.}
Let $C\succ0$ be a real positive-definite Gram (on the campaign,
the $\mu$-only dual CD Gram on the hole set $Y$).  The
\emph{graph resolvent} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:graphR}
R \;=\; C(I+C)^{-1}
 \;=\; (I+C^{-1})^{-1}
 \;=\; I-(I+C)^{-1}.
\end{equation}
The first form is the r407 intertwiner; the second is the
r356 dual resolvent of the L-ensemble kernel $E=C^{-1}$
(r409 graph identity).  Well-definedness, $0\prec R\prec I$, and
the spectral law $\operatorname{eig}(R)=\lambda/(1+\lambda)$ are
finite algebra \gr{lean-verified}.  The half-shift identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:halfshift}
R-\half I
 \;=\;
 \half\,(I+C)^{-1}(C-I)
\end{equation}
is an equality of matrices.  Because $(I+C)^{-1}\succ0$, Sylvester
gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:inertr}
\operatorname{ind}_{-}(R-\half I)
 \;=\;
 \operatorname{ind}_{-}(C-I).
\end{equation}
The factorization $I-C^{-1}=C^{-1}(C-I)$ is \emph{not} a
congruence.  The true sandwich is $C^{-1}(C-I)C^{-1}$, equivalently
the simultaneous eigenbasis, and yields the M\"obius law
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mobius}
\operatorname{ind}_{-}(C-I)
 \;=\;
 \operatorname{ind}_{-}(I-C^{-1}).
\end{equation}
If a rectangular factor satisfies $\Tfrak^{*}\Tfrak=C^{-1}$
(r409: the min-norm Birkhoff extension $\Tfrak_{0}$), the
energy split is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:esplit}
\|\Tfrak\|
 \;=\;
 1\big/\sqrt{\lambda_{\min}(C)},
\qquad
\|\Tfrak\|\le 1
 \;\iff\;
 C\succeq I,
\end{equation}
and ``at most one singular value $>1$'' is
$\operatorname{ind}_{-}(I-\Tfrak^{*}\Tfrak)\le1$, which is
\eqref{eq:inertr}--\eqref{eq:mobius} \gr{lean-verified}.

\section{What a proof would give, and what it would not}\label{sec:status}

A proof of Problem~\ref{prob:minimal} for the unbounded family
would give, in order:
$R\succ\half I$
by Theorem~\ref{thm:haynsworth} \gr{lean-verified finite algebra};
then $\Rd\succ\half I$ once $q^{\dagger}<1$, equivalently $\Ld$,
by Proposition~\ref{prop:A}(A3),(A5)
\gr{theorem-grade and lean-verified};
$\Ld\;\Rightarrow\;\Ls\wedge\mathrm{Terminal}$
and
$\Ls\wedge\mathrm{Terminal}\;\Rightarrow$
positivity of every bordered window matrix through the cap
\gr{Lean, sorry-free on the reconstruction layer, axiom audit
\texttt{RH/Audit.lean}}.  That is window-local master positivity.

chain without a separate terminal input.  Neither is an asymptotic
law, a bound on the Riemann $\xi$-function, or a statement about
the Riemann hypothesis in either direction.

What is on the table now is a finite census (at most $85$ windows
for the hole kernel, $75$ plus one deep row for the border, four

for the two-rank dichotomy, $181$ windows for the Pr\"ufer

sorry-free R$^{\dagger}$ Lean layer.  No Riemann--Hilbert
steepest-descent analysis has been carried out.  The
equilibrium-measure limit, the Gamma parametrix, and the error
operator $E$ are open, and they are no longer the cheapest target.
Comparison inputs show that the identities survive source
destruction while positivity and the saturated partition do not
